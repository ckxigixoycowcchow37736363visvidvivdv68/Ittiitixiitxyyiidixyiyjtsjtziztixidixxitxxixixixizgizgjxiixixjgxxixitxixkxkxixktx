Quick guide

Use these options to keep totals accurate.

1. Add savings to Alinma
Action: Add money / income · To: Alinma Personal
2. Allocate money to NT or FT
Action: Transfer · From: Alinma Personal · To: Barq — NT or Barq — FT
3. Spend money
Action: Spend from a bucket · choose the account that paid and the bucket. The account balance decreases.
4. Record an existing balance
Action: Set account balance · useful for your starting balance.
Tip: Set your current account balances once, then record every deposit, transfer and purchase. Your data is stored locally on this device.
